# OpenRGD Labs — Test benches & experimental equipment

Workspace for **physical experiments, bench instrumentation and reproducible test evidence** supporting Italia Robotica and OpenRGD projects (including Paky-01 and Paky-02).

## Scope

- [Laboratory equipment inventory](laboratory/equipment.md): available instruments, reported specifications, unknowns and procurement priorities.
- Future test-bench procedures, controlled measurements, and experiment reports can be added as separate, dated records.

## Boundaries

- This is **not** the canonical OpenRGD specification, an actuator-control system, a robot's as-built inventory, or a replacement for the OpenRGD Console.
- Machine-specific hardware truth and calibration live in their respective robot repositories; cross-reference test evidence rather than silently replicating it.
- Public documentation must exclude credentials, serial numbers that could expose ownership, personal purchase records, detailed site security data, or precise location.
- All equipment specifications are *reported or manufacturer-advertised* until instrument identity and ratings have been checked against the physical unit or official documentation.
- No repository content authorizes powered tests, physical actuator movement, or bypass of safety precautions.

## Evidence convention

- `OPERATOR_REPORTED`: identified from user statements, order screenshot, or earlier operator notes; not independently verified.
- `MANUFACTURER_ADVERTISED`: specification from a listing or datasheet; validation and revision still required.
- `PHYSICALLY_VERIFIED`: measured or directly inspected and evidenced, with test date/instrument/operator.
- `PLANNED`: not owned; target specification only.
